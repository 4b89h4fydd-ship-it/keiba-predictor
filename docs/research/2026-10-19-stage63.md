## Six independent research systems

arvexq/research/stage63 contains ability.py, running_style.py, pace.py,
probability.py, marks.py and tickets.py. experiment.py composes them offline.
All production v3 prediction modules remain untouched.

- Ability: full-history mean; recent-five weighted mean; fixed strength-three
  shrinkage. No outcomes or odds enter features.
- Running style: observed first-corner quartile rates; full history versus
  most recent five starts. Invalid/missing observations stay unknown.
- Pace: four fixed stress hypotheses versus neutral. Equal weights are
  assumptions, not calibrated scenario frequencies.
- Probability: stable Plackett-Luce mixture; temperatures 0.5, 1, 2.
  Re-normalizes remaining runners instead of subtracting nearly-one floats.
- Marks: unvalidated, returns None. No unsupported product marks.
- Tickets: independent top10 research combinations; no buying recommendation.

## Same-data exploratory results

NAR 2026-10-16, 36 total races; 25 fully scored (69.44%), 11 unscored.
All seven methods use the same 25 races. Each race costs a hypothetical
10 combinations × 100 yen. Lower logloss and Brier are better.

| Method | Winner Top1 | Winner Top3 | Logloss | Brier | Trifecta hits | ROI |
|---|---:|---:|---:|---:|---:|---:|
| Baseline | 11/25 | 17/25 | 2.181875 | 0.870676 | 4/25 | 32.72% |
| Recent5 | 10/25 | 16/25 | 2.172502 | 0.867478 | 3/25 | 18.36% |
| Recent5 + shrinkage | 11/25 | 15/25 | 2.237549 | 0.883092 | 5/25 | 102.52% |
| Recent5 running style | 11/25 | 15/25 | 2.182232 | 0.870813 | 4/25 | 32.72% |
| Neutral pace | 12/25 | 17/25 | 2.174121 | 0.868950 | 4/25 | 32.72% |
| Temperature 0.5 | 11/25 | 17/25 | 2.061847 | 0.839394 | 4/25 | 32.72% |
| Temperature 2 | 11/25 | 17/25 | 2.250048 | 0.886017 | 4/25 | 32.72% |

Changing tactical corrections or probability sharpness does not improve
trifecta results in this sample. Recent style rates reduce winner Top3.
Shrinkage increases trifecta hits but worsens both probability scores.
Its two largest returns account for 82.09% of all return.
The all-36-race hit denominators are 4/36 (11.11%) baseline and 5/36 (13.89%)
shrinkage, distinct from 4/25 (16%) and 5/25 (20%) among scored races.
No best arm is selected. Multiple comparisons and prior outcome inspection
make every result exploratory. Neither 80% nor 90% hit-rate targets are met;
102.52% on reused data does not establish long-term profitable performance.

## Forward protocol and honest provenance

forward.py adds write-once protocol/forecast receipts, SHA-256 binding of
plan/code/input/predictions, exact forecast recomputation, future post-time
checks, development-date exclusion, NAR/JRA isolation, roster checks and narrow
feature whitelists. Existing local receipt code is not replaced or weakened.

Candidate window: 2026-10-20 through 2026-11-18 JST, all available NAR saved cards.
No automatic data capture or scheduled task is installed. Uses files from the
existing acquisition path only; no replacement provider or race-data request.
Retain missing races in coverage and all-race denominators. No interim tuning,
arm selection, early stopping or automatic production promotion. Report at least
100 scored races across 10 racing dates before interpreting stability; if the
fixed window lacks that evidence, report insufficient evidence, not success.
The plan includes all 36 development race IDs and excludes dates through October19.

```bash
python scripts/arvexq_stage63_forward.py freeze --plan PLAN.json --input SAVED_RACE.json --output NEW_RECEIPT.json
python scripts/arvexq_stage63_forward.py verify --plan PLAN.json --input NEW_RECEIPT.json
```

IMPORTANT: the verifier always returns independentlyVerifiedPreoff=False and
independentEvaluationEligible=False. A checksum, local capturedAt or supplied
boolean cannot prove historical availability. An authenticated external witness
must bind plan, inputs and forecasts before each off time, and its authoritative
server observation time must be checked independently. Git commit author dates
are not such proof. No independent real-race receipt was available this stage.
The plan itself also needs an externally witnessed preregistration before use.
The framework is preparation, not a completed independent validation system.
